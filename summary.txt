World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026

1. Ukraine: Two days of US-mediated talks with Russia in Abu Dhabi ended with a prisoner-exchange deal US envoy Jared Kushner called the "biggest" yet; President Zelensky said it could be 1,000 for 1,000 or up to 1,200. Russian negotiator Vladimir Medinsky said Moscow seeks a "new, modern Yalta". He and Steve Witkoff were to see Putin in Moscow next. (Reuters, France 24)

2. Climate: Leaders opened the COP31 summit in Antalya, Turkey. UN chief Antonio Guterres urged a "climate justice compact" and windfall taxes on fossil-fuel firms, warning current plans mean over 2.5C of warming. (UN News)

3. US: AP projected Democrat Scott Colom beat Republican Senator Cindy Hyde-Smith in Mississippi, 49.2% to 48.7% with 99% counted, the state's first Black senator since Reconstruction and Democrats' eighth Senate pickup. (AP via ABC News)

4. Business: Nvidia's board met to weigh CEO Jensen Huang's future, Reuters reported, citing a source, after the New York Times reported his relationship with an executive reporting to him; shares fell over 8% after hours. (Reuters, NYT, Forbes)

5. Iran: Chief negotiator Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf told Al Jazeera the Strait of Hormuz will "never return" to its status before the eight-month US-Israel war on Iran, and that Tehran awaits a US reply to its seven conditions for ending it. (Al Jazeera)

6. Spain: The first general strike in 14 years, over housing costs, drew about 120,000 marchers in Madrid and 50,000+ in Barcelona, where masked groups clashed with police, before the 29 November election. (BBC)

7. Gaza: Israeli strikes killed at least seven Palestinians as the UN appealed for tents after floods. (Al Jazeera)

8. Pakistan: Eleven police were feared killed in an attack in Kohat, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, a rescue group said. (Reuters)

9. Kenya discharged the last patient of its Ebola outbreak, imported from DR Congo, starting a 42-day countdown to an Ebola-free declaration on 23 December. (WHO)
