World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026

1. Ukraine: US-mediated Russia-Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi ended after two days with a deal on a prisoner exchange US envoy Jared Kushner called the "biggest" yet; President Zelensky said it could be 1,000 for 1,000 or up to 1,200. Russian negotiator Vladimir Medinsky said Moscow seeks a "new, modern Yalta". Kushner and Steve Witkoff were to see Putin in Moscow next. (Reuters, France 24)

2. Climate: Leaders opened the COP31 summit in Antalya, Turkey. UN chief Antonio Guterres urged a "climate justice compact" and windfall taxes on fossil-fuel firms, warning current plans point to more than 2.5C of warming. (UN News)

3. US: AP projected Democrat Scott Colom beat Republican Senator Cindy Hyde-Smith in Mississippi, 49.2% to 48.7% with 99% counted, the state's first Black senator since Reconstruction and Democrats' eighth Senate pickup. (AP via ABC News)

4. Business: Nvidia's board met to weigh CEO Jensen Huang's future, Reuters reported, citing a source, after the New York Times reported he had a relationship with an executive who reports to him. Nvidia shares fell more than 8% after hours. (Reuters, NYT, Forbes)

5. Iran: Chief negotiator Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf told Al Jazeera the Strait of Hormuz will "never return" to its pre-war status and that Tehran awaits a US reply to its seven conditions for ending the war. (Al Jazeera)

6. Spain: The first general strike in 14 years, over housing costs, drew about 120,000 marchers in Madrid and more than 50,000 in Barcelona, where masked groups clashed with police, ahead of the 29 November election. (BBC)

7. Gaza: Israeli strikes killed at least seven Palestinians as the UN appealed for tents after floods. (Al Jazeera)

8. Pakistan: Eleven police were feared killed in an attack in Kohat, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, a rescue group said. (Reuters)

9. Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient, starting a 42-day countdown to being declared Ebola-free on 23 December. (WHO)
